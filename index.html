How do I order?

Tap "Order on WhatsApp" on the outfit you like. A message with the outfit name and price opens in WhatsApp. Send it, and we'll confirm your size and colour.

What if the size doesn't fit?

You can exchange your size within 5 days. Just message us on WhatsApp.

Do you have plus sizes?

Yes. Many styles come in sizes up to 3XL+. Tell us your size and we'll show you what fits.

Do you deliver outside Hathras?

Yes, we ship across India and worldwide. Ask us on WhatsApp for delivery options to your city.

Visit our store in Naya Ganj, or book a video call (₹300, adjusted in your bill) and we'll show you the outfit live.
